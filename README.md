# Eternal Vowz (E&V) — 3D Digital Wedding Invitation & RSVP Platform

Bespoke 3D interactive digital wedding invitations with real-time RSVP tracking, personalized WhatsApp guest links, and a Smart Conditional Card Builder — priced at a flat **₹99 per card** with 1-month (30-day) active hosting.

## Signature Features

- **5 Curated Animated Wedding Card Designs (Flat ₹99 / Card):**
  1. **Pearl Botanical Veil (`Layla & Omar`)** — Cinematic Frosted-Blur Veil reveal that melts into HD on tap (`/invite/layla-omar-veil`)
  2. **Shahi Palace Gates (`Amira & Rayyan`)** — 3D Carved Palace Double-Door Gates reveal (`/invite/amira-rayyan-palace`)
  3. **Tuscan Sunset Rose (`Sophia & Alexander`)** — 3D Embossed Merlot & Gold Lifafa (`/invite/sophia-alexander`)
  4. **Royal Mughal Arch (`Zoya & Ayaan`)** — 3D Embossed Emerald & 24k Gold Lifafa (`/invite/zoya-ayaan-nikah`)
  5. **Celestial Starlight (`Fatima & Hamza`)** — 3D Embossed Midnight Sapphire & Gold Lifafa (`/invite/fatima-hamza-starlight`)
- **Smart Conditional Card Builder:** Fill only the essentials (Couple Names, Date & Venue) — optional sections (Schedule, Modest Dress Code, Halal Banquet Menu, Couple Story, Gift Registry, Stay Info, FAQ) automatically hide unless enabled.
- **Host Dashboard (`/dashboard`):** Manage all wedding cards, live read receipts, RSVP headcount, Halal catering summary, CSV export, 30-day validity countdowns, and post-purchase Priority WhatsApp Support.
- **Security & Legal Suite:** Strict HTTP Security Headers (`next.config.mjs`), server-enforced ₹99 payment order & HMAC-SHA256 verification route (`/api/payment`), plus dedicated `/privacy`, `/terms`, `/refund`, and `/contact` pages.
